Privacy Policy

Moonpetal Apothecary · Bit Serenity Studios LLC · Last updated July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bit Serenity Studios LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Moonpetal Apothecary (the "Game"). By playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

Information we handle

  • Game data on your device. Your progress, settings, and in-game currencies are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless your device or platform backs it up.
  • Device & diagnostic information. We and our service providers may process technical data such as device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and crash or performance logs to keep the Game running and to fix problems.
  • Gameplay analytics. We may process anonymous or pseudonymous events (for example, levels started or completed) to understand how the Game is used and to improve it. These events are not used to identify you personally.
  • Purchases. If you buy anything in the Game, the payment is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred; we do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Advertising. If the Game shows ads, advertising partners may use a device advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads. You can reset or limit this identifier in your device settings.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Game.
  • To improve gameplay, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • To process and validate purchases.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our terms.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the Game (for example, cloud hosting, crash reporting, analytics, and advertising partners), who may process it only on our behalf;
  • App stores (Apple, Google) for purchases and distribution; and
  • Authorities where required by law.

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking via your device's advertising settings.
  • Delete the Game to remove locally stored game data from your device.
  • Contact us to ask what information we hold about you or to request deletion, where applicable law provides that right.

Data retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.
